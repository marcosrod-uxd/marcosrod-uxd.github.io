Thesis Project

Summary

My thesis focused on improving the new user experience for the video game League of Legends through a UI overhaul. I removed unnecessary UI elements and created new elements to guide the user through each step of the tutorial. After user testing, my results showed that my prototype successfully improved UI clarity scores by 26% and tutorial completion time by 19%.

The Problem

League of Legends is an incredibly popular video game with over 180 million monthly players. However, the player base has been dwindling since 2022. After sending out a survey to current players, I found that over 72% of participants rated their new player experience as negative or neutral. I believe that by improving the tutorial experience, new players will have a more positive first impression and be more willing to continue playing. 

My Approach

I utilized the double diamond design process for this project. My research plan was focused on understanding potential new players and honing in on their pain points and learning styles. 

Discover: User Research

I conducted in-depth user research to understand the needs and pain points of new players. My methods included:

Current Player User Survey

I sent out a survey to current League of Legends players and asked about their experiences as a new player.

New Player Cognitive Walkthrough

I sat down with new players and had them walk through the tutorial and recorded their thoughts and gameplay. 

New Player User Interview

Before and after the cognitive walkthrough I interviewed the new players about their interests, experience level, feelings about the tutorial, and pain points. 

Define: Findings

Through my research I found that new users suffer from the following issues with the current tutorial: 

Cognitive Overload

The existing tutorial bombards new players with too much information at once. New players report feelings of confusion and frustration.

Overwhelming UI

The user interface is visually overwhelming, making it difficult for new players to find and understand necessary elements.

Insufficient Guidance

The tutorial lacks clear, step-by-step instructions and feedback. New players reported feeling lost and did not pick up on current tutorial cues.

Based on these findings, I created a user persona, Alex, a 19-year-old college student who prefers casual games and is easily frustrated by complex interfaces. The goal was to design a tutorial for a user like Alex.

Develop: Prototype

I designed new UI elements using Figma to address the identified problems.
Key objectives of the prototype:

Simplify Existing UI

The prototype reduces visual clutter and prioritizes essential elements.

Enhance Guidance

Added an objective tracker to notify players of their current task.

Minimizable Assistance

Users can press a key to access or minimize a sidebar of short videos that demonstrate specific concepts.

Deliver: Results

I conducted A/B testing with 11 participants who had no prior League of Legends experience. The Experimental Group used my Figma prototype before the original tutorial, while the Control Group only used the original tutorial.
The following metrics were gathered:

Efficiency

Players completed the tutorial 16% faster, located UI elements with greater accuracy, and reported a 19% increase in the ease of finding those elements.

Clarity

Subjective ratings of in-game UI clarity increased by 29%, with the prototype UI itself receiving a mean clarity rating of 4.17 out of 5.

Comprehension

Overall understanding of abilities increased by 83%, and self-rated understanding rose by 66%. However mean scores were middling (2.67 out of 5 for experimental), and for users with little to no experience with games the prototype did not seem to be of any benefit.

Prototype Helpfulness

The prototype was rated as helpful in locating UI elements, receiving a mean score of 3.5 out of 5. For users with little to no experience with games the prototype did not seem to be of any benefit.

Conclusion

My prototype successfully enhanced the new player experience by reducing cognitive load, improving UI clarity, and increasing comprehension of core game mechanics. This research demonstrates the value of a user-centered design approach in creating a more positive and effective onboarding experience for a complex product.
